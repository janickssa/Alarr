1Batch 1 · Identity & design system

Built on Nocturne in dark mode. Health colours are the only saturated values in the UI. Service accents are kept at low chroma, so they never read as a status.

1aName & logo, three proposals
Recommended
Quorum

A Proxmox word meaning the cluster agrees it is healthy. Short, technical and calm. The mark is three nodes holding one core.

Hearth

Warmer and friendlier for the household. "Home" as a place, with a small flame for "running". It reads less technical.

Lantern

A light you carry off the network and back in over the VPN. It matches the glow-not-flood accent, and the name is broader than homelab.

Quorum
Lockup used in the sidebar, login and splash. The rest of this board uses Quorum.
